# Study 1: Community Survey on Metadata Requirements

This folder contains the materials and data for Study 1, a community 
survey evaluating the perceived relevance of the proposed lifecycle 
metadata fields for declarative mappings. Study 1 corresponds to 
Chapter 5, Section 5.3 of the thesis.

## Purpose

Study 1 validated the relevance of the 37 proposed metadata fields 
across two mapping-related tasks (mapping discovery for reuse, and 
mapping quality assessment) with practitioners from the semantic web 
and knowledge engineering community.

## Contents

- `questionnaire/` — The survey instrument as deployed via Google Forms.
- `raw-data/` — Anonymised participant responses.
- `results/` — Processed tables corresponding to thesis Tables 5.2–5.7.
