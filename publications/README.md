# Publications

This folder contains the peer-reviewed publications associated with the LMMD thesis. The papers are listed below in chronological order, matching the file numbering in this folder. Each paper is also referenced in Section 1.5 of the thesis.

## Contents

1. **Towards a Unified Metadata Model for Semantic and Data Mappings.** Alzahrani, S., & O'Sullivan, D. (2022). *Proceedings of the Ontology Matching Workshop (OM 2022), co-located with the International Semantic Web Conference (ISWC 2022).* Early conceptual paper exploring the feasibility of a unified metadata model for mappings. Provided the initial insights that informed the lifecycle-based model later adopted in MMV.

2. **What Metadata Is Needed for Semantic and Data Mappings?** Alzahrani, S., & O'Sullivan, D. (2023). *Proceedings of the IEEE 17th International Conference on Semantic Computing (IEEE ICSC 2023).* Reports the community survey identifying key metadata requirements for semantic and data mappings. Forms the empirical foundation for the Mapping Metadata Vocabulary (MMV) design. Corresponds to Study 1 in the thesis.

3. **MetaMap: Facilitating Mapping Reuse and Lifecycle Documentation.** Alzahrani, S., & O'Sullivan, D. (2024). *Proceedings of the IEEE 18th International Conference on Semantic Computing (IEEE ICSC 2024).* Introduces an early prototype of MetaSEMAP supporting mapping reuse and lifecycle documentation, motivating the need for structured mapping metadata.

4. **MetaSEMAP: Usability Evaluation for Metadata Annotation Across the Mapping Lifecycle.** Alzahrani, S., & O'Sullivan, D. (2025). *Proceedings of the International Workshop on Users and Knowledge Graphs (UKG 2025), co-located with the SEMANTiCS Conference.* Reports a user study evaluating the usability and learnability of structured lifecycle-based metadata annotation using the MetaSEMAP tool. Corresponds to Study 2 in the thesis.

5. **Guiding LLM-Generated Mappings with Lifecycle-Based Metadata: An Early Evaluation.** Alzahrani, S., & O'Sullivan, D. (2025). *Proceedings of the KG-NeSy Workshop, co-located with SEMANTiCS 2025.* Presents an early evaluation of using lifecycle metadata to guide LLM-based mapping generation. Provided the conceptual basis for Study 5 in the thesis.

6. **MMV: A Lifecycle-Based Ontology for Declarative Mapping Projects.** Alzahrani, S., & O'Sullivan, D. (2026). *Proceedings of the IEEE 20th International Conference on Semantic Computing (IEEE ICSC 2026).* (in press). Presents MMV (Mapping Metadata Vocabulary), a lifecycle-based ontology designed to represent metadata for declarative mapping projects across different mapping types. The paper describes the design of MMV, illustrates its instantiation, and outlines the planned expert evaluation reported as Study 3 in the thesis.
